## Professional interpretation

The strongest distinction is the combination of product thinking and implementation: noticing a specific need, designing the interaction, handling the data model and checking the delivered behavior. The site now shows that through LockIn, Setline and VIDEO-RESCUE rather than suggesting seniority or AI expertise.

Tutoring, multicultural customer service and the Spain–China education path give useful context for communication and collaboration. These existing experiences are connected to the working style; no private psychological material, invented hobbies, personality diagnosis, client metrics or user outcomes were used. The claim is supported product/research work, not superiority over every other candidate or measured hiring conversion.

## Review measurements and validation

At a 1440 × 1050 local browser viewport, the Spanish Home changed from 1,107 to 859 words and from 10,637 to 6,499 CSS pixels in document height. The research overview changed from 504 to 362 words. These describe the rendered documents in that environment; they are not a usability study, performance benchmark or recruiter outcome.

The original palette, typography family, genuine product visuals and familiar page patterns were retained. Desktop/mobile screenshots of Home, About, Research, Privacy and all three cases were inspected, along with both PDF renders and social cards.

Validation passed: production static export; formatting, lint and strict TypeScript; nine content tests; 26 desktop/mobile browser tests including WCAG AA automated checks, direct routes, language switching, image loading, legacy bookmarks and CV links; internal links/assets across 19 HTML files. Additional browser checks confirmed overview layouts at 320, 375, 780 and 1024 pixels and mobile-menu selection/Escape behavior. Automated accessibility checks do not replace a complete manual audit.

An attempted framework-generated social-image route failed static export. The final implementation uses committed static PNGs produced by the existing Playwright dependency, with no new service or dependency.

Publication follows the already-authorized GitHub Pages workflow. Deployment completion and public page/PDF readback are verified separately after push.

## Useful next evidence

A short sanitized walkthrough of each app and a documented usability session would add evidence of how other people experience the products. Those are future evidence opportunities; the present website makes no conversion, adoption or user-impact claim.
